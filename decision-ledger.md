## Maintaining the ledger

- Add an entry when a new design question emerges; do not convert an assessment into a decision without named-owner confirmation.
- Record evidence references rather than restating evidence.
- Mark an entry `confirmed` only after the owner confirms the selected option and rationale.
- Mark superseded entries with the replacement decision; do not delete them.
- The eventual ADR cites the confirmed entries it records and identifies unresolved entries as open questions.
